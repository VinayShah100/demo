The Science of Cellular Energy

Understanding NAD+ and its Powerful Partner, Resveratrol

NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide) is a crucial coenzyme found in every cell of your body. It plays a vital role in:

  • Converting nutrients into cellular energy
  • Activating sirtuins, proteins that regulate cellular health
  • Repairing damaged DNA
  • Regulating circadian rhythms

Resveratrol enhances NAD+ effectiveness by activating sirtuin pathways, creating a powerful synergy for cellular health and longevity.
